# Draftly v1.0.0

The first public release of Draftly — a local-first, privacy-native note-taking app for your desktop. Free and open source under the MIT License.

## What's new

- **Seven note templates** — Plain Note, Meeting Notes, Release / Changelog, Workflow / SOP, Email Draft, Book Draft, and Ledger.
- **AI writing assistant** — rewrite a selection in six tones or summarize it, using your own Claude, Gemini, Grok, or Groq API key.
- **Five themes** — Fox, Raven, and Moose (dark); Penguin and Polar Bear (light).
- **Pinboard and All Notes** — pin important notes, and browse everything as cards with filters, search, and sorting.
- **PDF export** and optional **Google Drive backup** to your own account.
- **Linux, macOS and Windows** — a `.deb` package, a plain Linux tarball, one universal macOS app for Apple Silicon and Intel, and a portable Windows `.exe`.
- No account, no telemetry. Your notes stay on your machine.
