# Draftly v1.1.0

Deleted notes are no longer gone for good. This release adds a Trash, so you can bring a note back if you delete it by mistake.

## What's new

- **Trash.** Deleting a note now moves it to the Trash, found at the bottom of the sidebar. From there you can restore it or delete it forever. Notes left in the Trash are removed automatically after 30 days.
- **Maximize button on macOS.** The green window button now works, so you can make Draftly full screen or zoom it to fill the screen, like any other Mac app.
- Your existing notes are kept as they are when you update. Nothing moves to the Trash on its own.
